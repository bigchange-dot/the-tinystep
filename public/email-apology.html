사과/정정 이메일 템플릿 10선

사과는 길게 변명하기보다 “인정 → 영향 최소화 → 해결/대안”을 짧게 주는 게 가장 프로페셔널해요.

사과의 골든 포맷

  • Sorry for … / I apologize for …
  • This was due to … (짧게, 필요할 때만)
  • To fix this, we will … / Here is the corrected …
  • Thank you for your patience/understanding
1) 답장 지연 사과

Subject: Apologies for the Delayed Response

Hi [Name],
Sorry for the delayed response. Thank you for your patience.
Regarding [topic], [your answer/update].
Best,
[Your Name]

2) 일정 변경 사과 + 대안

Subject: Apologies – Rescheduling [Meeting]

Hello [Name],
I apologize for the inconvenience, but I need to reschedule our meeting.
Could we move it to [Option A] or [Option B]?
Thank you for understanding.
[Your Name]

3) 오정보 정정

Subject: Correction – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Correction to my previous message: [correct info] (not [wrong info]).
Sorry for the confusion, and thank you for your understanding.
Best regards,
[Your Name]

4) 첨부 누락 사과

Subject: Apologies – Missing Attachment

Hi [Name],
Sorry—I forgot to attach the file in my previous email.
Please find the attachment included here: [file name].
Thanks,
[Your Name]

5) 실수 인정 + 해결책

Subject: Apologies – [Issue]

Hello [Name],
I’m sorry for the mistake on [issue]. We have corrected it and updated [item] accordingly.
Please let me know if anything else needs to be adjusted.
Sincerely,
[Your Name]

6) 원인 설명(짧게) + 재발방지

Subject: Apology and Update

Hi [Name],
Apologies for [issue]. This happened due to [short reason].
We’re putting [prevention step] in place to avoid this going forward.
Thank you for your understanding.
[Your Name]

7) 배송/납기 지연 안내

Subject: Update – Delay in Delivery

Hello [Name],
I’m sorry to inform you that delivery will be delayed until [new date].
We’re working to minimize the impact and will keep you updated.
Thank you for your patience.
[Your Name]

8) 상대 불편 인정

Subject: Apologies for the Inconvenience

Hi [Name],
I understand this may have caused inconvenience, and I’m sorry about that.
To address it, [action].
Thank you for your patience.
[Your Name]

9) 강한 사과(중요 이슈)

Subject: Sincere Apologies – [Issue]

Dear [Name],
Please accept my sincere apologies for [issue].
We take this seriously and are taking immediate steps: [steps].
I appreciate your understanding.
[Your Name]

10) 사과 + 다음 일정 확정

Subject: Apology and Next Steps

Hello [Name],
Sorry again for [issue]. To move forward, we will [next step] by [date].
Thank you for your patience.
[Your Name]

사과 표현 10개

  • Sorry for the delayed response.
  • I apologize for the inconvenience.
  • Sorry for the confusion.
  • Please accept my sincere apologies.
  • Thank you for your patience.
  • We’ve corrected the issue.
  • We’re taking steps to prevent this from happening again.
  • I understand this may have caused inconvenience.
  • Here is the corrected information.
  • Please let me know if you have any concerns.
