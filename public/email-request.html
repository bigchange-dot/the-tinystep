비즈니스 요청 이메일 템플릿 10선

견적/자료/검토/일정 요청은 “명확 + 정중 + 기한 제시” 3가지만 지켜도 상대가 훨씬 편하게 읽습니다.

빠른 구조(복붙용)

  • 요청: Could you please… / Would it be possible to…
  • 맥락: We’re currently… / For our review…
  • 기한: By (date) / By EOD (day)
  • 감사: Thanks in advance / I appreciate your help
1) 견적 요청

Subject: Request for a Quote – [Item/Service]

Hello [Name],
Could you please provide a quote for [item/service]? If possible, please include lead time and payment terms.
We would appreciate it if you could share it by [date].
Thank you,
[Your Name]

2) 자료/문서 요청

Subject: Request for Documents – [Project]

Hi [Name],
Could you share the following documents for our review?
- [Doc 1], [Doc 2]
If you can send them by [date/time], that would be great.
Best,
[Your Name]

3) 검토 요청(상사/고객)

Subject: Request for Review – [Topic]

Dear [Name],
Would you be able to review the attached [file] and share your feedback?
If you’re available, I’d appreciate your comments by [date].
Sincerely,
[Your Name]

4) 빠른 확인 요청(가벼운 톤)

Subject: Quick Check – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Quick question—could you confirm [point] for me?
Thanks!
[Your Name]

5) 우선순위/긴급도 조절

Subject: Time-Sensitive Request – [Topic]

Hello [Name],
This is time-sensitive due to [reason]. Could you help us by [date/time]?
If the timeline is difficult, please let me know what’s feasible.
Thank you,
[Your Name]

6) 옵션 제시(상대 부담 줄이기)

Subject: Request – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Would it be possible to [request]? If not, [alternative] would also work.
Please let me know what you prefer.
Best regards,
[Your Name]

7) 미팅 요청

Subject: Meeting Request – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Would you be available for a [15/30]-minute meeting to discuss [topic]?
I’m available [option A] or [option B]. Please let me know what works for you.
Thanks,
[Your Name]

8) 협조 요청(부서/동료)

Subject: Request for Support – [Task]

Hi team,
Could you support [task] by [date]? This will help us [impact].
Thank you in advance.
[Your Name]

9) 요청 범위 명확히

Subject: Request – Scope Confirmation

Hello [Name],
Just to confirm the scope: we need [exact items] (excluding [excluded]).
Could you proceed based on this and share an ETA?
Thank you,
[Your Name]

10) 마감 전 리마인드(요청 + 배려)

Subject: Friendly Reminder – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Just a friendly reminder about [request]. Would you be able to share it by [date]?
If you need more time, please let me know.
Thank you,
[Your Name]

자주 쓰는 요청 표현 12개

  • Could you please send/share/confirm …?
  • Would it be possible to …?
  • Could you take a look and let me know your thoughts?
  • If possible, could you … by [date]?
  • When would be a good time to …?
  • Please let me know if you have any questions.
  • Thanks in advance for your help.
  • I appreciate your support on this.
  • If that timeline is difficult, please share an alternative.
  • Could you clarify [point]?
  • Could you provide an update on [topic]?
  • We’d be grateful if you could prioritize this.
