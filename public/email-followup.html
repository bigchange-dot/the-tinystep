미회신 리마인드(Follow-up) 템플릿 10선

리마인드는 공격적으로 보이지 않게 “배려 + 선택지 + 다음 액션”을 같이 주는 게 핵심이에요.

리마인드 3단 구조

  • 상기: Just following up on …
  • 요청: Could you share an update / confirm …?
  • 여지: If you need more time, please let me know.
1) 가장 무난한 follow-up

Subject: Following up – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Just following up on my previous message regarding [topic].
Do you have any updates you could share?
Thanks,
[Your Name]

2) 기한을 다시 확인

Subject: Quick Follow-up – [Topic]

Hello [Name],
Could you please confirm whether we can expect an update by [date]?
If the timeline has changed, please let me know what works best.
Thank you,
[Your Name]

3) 매우 공손(상사/고객)

Subject: Gentle Reminder – [Topic]

Dear [Name],
I hope you’re doing well. I wanted to gently follow up on [topic].
When you have a moment, could you please advise on the next steps?
Sincerely,
[Your Name]

4) “혹시 놓쳤을까봐” 톤

Subject: Just Checking In – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Just checking in—sharing again in case it got buried in your inbox.
Could you please take a look when you get a chance?
Best,
[Your Name]

5) 다음 액션 제안(미팅)

Subject: Next Steps – [Topic]

Hello [Name],
To move this forward, would a quick [15]-minute call help?
I’m available [A] or [B]. Please let me know your preference.
Thanks,
[Your Name]

6) 의사결정 포인트를 좁혀주기

Subject: Follow-up – Decision Needed

Hi [Name],
To confirm, should we proceed with [Option A] or [Option B]?
Once confirmed, we can finalize by [date].
Thank you,
[Your Name]

7) 진행상황만 짧게 요청

Subject: Status Update – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Could you share a quick status update on [topic]? Even a short note is fine.
Thanks,
[Your Name]

8) 담당자 변경 확인

Subject: Follow-up – Point of Contact

Hello [Name],
Just checking—are you still the best contact for [topic]?
If not, could you please point me to the right person?
Best regards,
[Your Name]

9) 부드러운 “마감 임박”

Subject: Reminder – [Topic] (Due [date])

Hi [Name],
Friendly reminder that we’re aiming to close this by [date].
Could you share the update when possible?
Thanks in advance,
[Your Name]

10) 마지막 follow-up(정중)

Subject: Final Follow-up – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
This will be my final follow-up for now. Please let me know if you’d like to proceed or pause.
Thank you for your time,
[Your Name]

리마인드 표현 10개

  • Just following up on …
  • Do you have any updates on …?
  • When you have a moment, could you …?
  • In case it got buried in your inbox …
  • Could you confirm the timeline for …?
  • If you need more time, please let me know.
  • Would it help to jump on a quick call?
  • We’re aiming to finalize by …
  • Any guidance on next steps would be appreciated.
  • This will be my final follow-up for now.
