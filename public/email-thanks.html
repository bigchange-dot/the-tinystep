감사/칭찬 이메일 템플릿 10선

감사는 “무엇을 + 왜 도움이 됐는지”를 한 줄만 더 붙이면 진짜 진정성 있게 들려요.

1) 일반 감사

Subject: Thank You

Hi [Name],
Thank you for your help with [topic]. I really appreciate your support.
Best,
[Your Name]

2) 빠른 처리 감사

Subject: Thanks for the Quick Turnaround

Hello [Name],
Thanks for the quick turnaround on [item]. It helped us keep things on track.
Best regards,
[Your Name]

3) 미팅 후 감사

Subject: Thank You for Your Time

Hi [Name],
Thank you for meeting with me today. Your insights on [topic] were very helpful.
Looking forward to next steps.
[Your Name]

4) 고객/상사에게 공손

Subject: Thank You

Dear [Name],
Thank you for your guidance and support regarding [topic].
I appreciate your time and consideration.
Sincerely,
[Your Name]

5) 협업 칭찬

Subject: Great Work on [Project]

Hi team,
Great work on [project]. The way you handled [specific point] made a big difference.
Thanks everyone!
[Your Name]

6) 소개/연결 감사

Subject: Thank You for the Introduction

Hello [Name],
Thank you for connecting me with [person]. I appreciate the introduction.
Best,
[Your Name]

7) 도움 받은 포인트 구체화

Subject: Thank You

Hi [Name],
Thanks again for your help. Your suggestion on [point] was especially useful.
I appreciate it.
[Your Name]

8) 마감 직전 지원 감사

Subject: Thanks for Your Support

Hello [Name],
Thank you for supporting us under a tight timeline. Your help with [task] was crucial.
Best regards,
[Your Name]

9) 승인/검토 감사

Subject: Thank You for Reviewing

Hi [Name],
Thank you for reviewing [document] and sharing your feedback. It was very helpful.
Best,
[Your Name]

10) 다음 액션 포함

Subject: Thank You – Next Steps

Hi [Name],
Thanks for your support on [topic]. As a next step, we’ll [action] by [date].
Best regards,
[Your Name]

  • Your insights were very helpful.
  • Thanks for taking the time to …
  • Thank you for your guidance.

  • This made a big difference.
