호텔 여행 영어 표현 30선

호텔은 정중하게 “Could I… / Could you… / Would it be possible…”를 쓰면 대부분 해결돼요.

자주 쓰는 단어: check-in, check-out, reservation, key card, housekeeping, room service

1) 체크인 할게요
I’d like to check in.
2) 예약했어요
I have a reservation under [name].
3) 여권 여기 있어요
Here is my passport.
4) 조식 포함인가요?
Is breakfast included?
5) 체크아웃 시간은 언제예요?
What time is check-out?
6) 레이트 체크아웃 가능해요?
Is late check-out available?
7) 키카드가 안 돼요
My key card isn’t working.
8) 방을 바꿀 수 있나요?
Could I change rooms?
9) 더 조용한 방이 있나요?
Do you have a quieter room?
10) 추가 수건 부탁해요
Could I get a couple of extra towels, please?
11) 물/어메니티 더 주세요
Could I get more water/amenities, please?
12) 하우스키핑 요청할게요
Could you send housekeeping, please?
13) 지금 청소 가능해요?
Could you clean the room now?
14) 나중에 청소해 주세요
Could you come back later?
15) 방에 문제가 있어요
There’s an issue with the room.
16) 에어컨이 안 돼요
The air conditioner isn’t working.
17) 와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요?
What’s the Wi-Fi password?
18) 와이파이가 느려요
The Wi-Fi is slow.
19) 룸서비스 가능해요?
Is room service available?
20) 수영장/헬스장 어디예요?
Where is the pool/gym?
21) 짐 맡길 수 있나요?
Could you store my luggage?
22) 택시 불러주세요
Could you call a taxi for me?
23) 근처 맛집 추천해요?
Could you recommend a good restaurant nearby?
24) 체크아웃 할게요
I’d like to check out.
25) 영수증 주세요
Could I get a receipt, please?
26) 이 청구가 맞나요?
Could you confirm this charge?
27) 보증금/디파짓이 있나요?
Is there a deposit?
28) 카드로 결제할게요
I’ll pay by card.
29) 늦게 도착해요
I’ll be arriving late.
30) 도움 주셔서 감사합니다
Thank you for your help.
