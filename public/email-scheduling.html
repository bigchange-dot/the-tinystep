일정 조율/변경 이메일 템플릿 10선

스케줄링은 “옵션 2~3개 제시 + 타임존(필요 시) + 캘린더 초대”가 핵심이에요.

자주 쓰는 표현

  • Would you be available …?
  • Could we reschedule to …?
  • Does [time] work for you?
  • I’ll send a calendar invite.
1) 미팅 제안(옵션 2개)

Subject: Meeting – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Would you be available for a [30]-minute meeting to discuss [topic]?
I’m available [Option A] or [Option B]. Please let me know what works for you.
Best,
[Your Name]

2) 시간만 확인(짧게)

Subject: Quick Scheduling Check

Hi [Name],
Does [time] work for you for [topic]?
Thanks!
[Your Name]

3) 일정 변경 요청

Subject: Reschedule Request – [Meeting]

Hello [Name],
Could we reschedule our meeting due to [reason]?
Would [Option A] or [Option B] work instead?
Thank you,
[Your Name]

4) 일정 확정 + 초대장

Subject: Confirmed – [Meeting]

Hi [Name],
Great—let’s meet on [date/time]. I’ll send a calendar invite shortly.
Looking forward to it.
[Your Name]

5) 취소(대안 제공)

Subject: Update – [Meeting]

Hello [Name],
Unfortunately, I need to cancel our meeting on [date].
Could we move it to [Option A] or [Option B]?
Apologies for the inconvenience.
[Your Name]

6) 타임존 포함

Subject: Meeting Time Confirmation (Time Zone)

Hi [Name],
Just to confirm, our meeting is scheduled for [date/time] ([KST/UTC/ET]).
Please let me know if you’d prefer a different time.
Best,
[Your Name]

7) 일정 후보 3개

Subject: Scheduling Options – [Topic]

Hi [Name],
Here are a few options for our meeting:
1) [A]
2) [B]
3) [C]
Which one works best for you?
Thanks,
[Your Name]

8) 짧게 시간만 변경

Subject: Time Change – [Meeting]

Hi [Name],
Could we move our meeting from [old time] to [new time]?
Please confirm if that works for you.
Thank you,
[Your Name]

9) 미팅 아젠다 포함

Subject: Meeting Agenda – [Topic]

Hello [Name],
To make our meeting efficient, the agenda is:
- [point 1]
- [point 2]
See you on [date/time].
Best,
[Your Name]

10) 일정 잡기 어려울 때(상대에게 공 던지기)

Subject: Scheduling – Your Availability

Hi [Name],
Could you share a few time slots that work for you next week?
I’ll adjust my schedule accordingly.
Thanks,
[Your Name]

스케줄링 표현 12개

  • Would you be available on …?
  • Does … work for you?
  • Could we reschedule to …?
  • I’ll send a calendar invite.
  • Please feel free to propose another time.
  • Let’s confirm the time zone.
  • Could you share your availability?
  • Looking forward to meeting you.
  • Thanks for your flexibility.
  • Apologies for the change.
  • Let’s finalize by …
  • See you then.
