미팅 일정 변경 영어: 자연스러운 문장 12개

일정 변경은 “사유 1줄 + 대안 2개 + 감사”가 가장 깔끔해요. 특히 대안 시간을 구체적으로 제시하면 상대가 답하기 쉬워집니다.

안전한 예시(복붙용)
Unfortunately, I have a conflict at that time. Could we reschedule?
I’m available on Tue 2–4 PM or Wed 10–12 AM. Please let me know what works best.
Sorry for the inconvenience, and thank you for your flexibility.
  • I have a conflict at that time. Could we reschedule?
  • Would it be possible to move the meeting to tomorrow?
  • Could we push it back by 30 minutes?
  • Could we bring it forward to 10 AM?
  • I’m available on Tue 2–4 PM or Wed 10–12 AM.
  • Please let me know which time works best for you.
  • Apologies for the inconvenience, and thank you for your flexibility.
  • If rescheduling is difficult, we can handle this over email.
  • Would a 15-minute quick call work instead?
  • Could we confirm the new time by EOD today?
  • Thanks again—this will help us stay on track.
  • I appreciate your understanding.
