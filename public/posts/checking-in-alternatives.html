“Just checking in”이 부담될 때: 팔로업 표현 12개

팔로업은 “재촉”이 아니라 “상황 정리”로 들리게 만드는 게 핵심이에요. 배경 1줄 + 질문 1개 + 선택지 구조가 가장 안전합니다.

안전한 팔로업 구조
• (배경) I wanted to follow up on the document I sent on Monday.
• (질문) Do you have any updates or feedback?
• (선택지) If you need more time, please let me know what timeline works best.
  • Following up on ~ — Following up on the proposal I shared last week.
  • Just wanted to confirm ~ — Just wanted to confirm if you had a chance to review it.
  • Do you have any updates? — Do you have any updates on the approval status?
  • Any feedback would be appreciated — Any feedback would be greatly appreciated.
  • When would be a good time? — When would be a good time to expect a response?
  • To keep things moving — To keep things moving, could you confirm by Friday?
  • Let me know if you need anything — Let me know if you need more info from my side.
  • If you need more time — If you need more time, please share a revised timeline.
  • Just checking whether ~ — Just checking whether the attachment came through.
  • Friendly reminder — A friendly reminder regarding the pending confirmation.
  • Are we still on track for ~? — Are we still on track for Friday’s deadline?
  • Happy to adjust — Happy to adjust if priorities have shifted—please advise.
