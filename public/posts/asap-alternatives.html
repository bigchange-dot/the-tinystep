ASAP이 무례하게 들릴 때: 대안 표현 12개

ASAP은 상황에 따라 “급하게 재촉한다”로 들릴 수 있어요. 대신 구체적인 기한과 이유(한 줄)를 함께 쓰면 훨씬 프로페셔널합니다.

핵심 원칙
① ASAP 대신 “언제까지”를 말하기 (by Friday / by 3 PM / within 2 hours)
② 이유를 한 줄 추가하기 (to finalize the report / to meet the deadline)
③ 필요하면 대안을 열어두기 (If that’s difficult, please suggest another time.)
  • by EOD today — Please share the file by EOD today.
  • by 3 PM (local time) — Could you confirm by 3 PM today?
  • by Friday — We’d appreciate it if you could respond by Friday.
  • within the next hour — I’ll send it within the next hour.
  • at your earliest convenience — Please reply at your earliest convenience. (조금 격식)
  • as soon as you can — When you have a moment, could you…?
  • when you get a chance — Let me know when you get a chance.
  • as soon as it’s available — Please share it as soon as it’s available.
  • by tomorrow afternoon — I’ll follow up by tomorrow afternoon.
  • at the latest, by Friday — At the latest, by Friday would be great.
  • to stay on track, by ~ — To stay on track, we need it by Friday.
  • if possible, by ~ — If possible, could you send it by EOD?

Tip: “ASAP”가 필요한 상황이면 기한을 더 구체화하고, 대안을 꼭 열어두면 압박감이 줄어듭니다.
