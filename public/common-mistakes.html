사과 격려 콩글리쉬

콩글리시/오역 교정표 30선

“단어는 맞는데 문장이 어색한” 경우가 많아서, 자주 쓰는 패턴을 통째로 자연스럽게 바꾼 예시야.

Before (어색/직역) After (자연/권장) 설명
Can we meeting tomorrow?
Can we meet tomorrow?
동사 형태 수정
I will contact you again.
I’ll follow up with you.
업무 리마인드 톤
Please understand me.
I appreciate your understanding.
부드러운 표현
I’m sorry to disturb you.
Sorry to bother you.
자연스러운 관용
I will share you the file.
I’ll share the file with you.
전치사 필요
Please check it and reply me.
Could you review it and let me know?
비즈니스 톤
I want to request you.
I’d like to ask for your help.
request 동사 주의
Thanks for your helping.
Thank you for your help.
help는 보통 명사
I’ll do my best.
I’ll do my best to [action].
뒤에 목적 넣기
We have a schedule.
We have a meeting scheduled.
문맥 명확화
Please give me a call.
Could you give me a call?
요청형으로 완화
I will do it ASAP.
I’ll take care of it as soon as possible.
자연스러운 문장
Let’s keep in touch.
Let’s stay in touch.
관용 표현
We are preparing now.
We’re working on it now.
업무 상황 표현
I will reflect it.
I’ll incorporate your feedback.
reflect 오해
The meeting was finished.
The meeting has ended.
자연스러운 완료
I’m very busy now.
I’m tied up at the moment.
부드러운 표현
I’ll send it to you later.
I’ll send it over shortly.
업무 톤
Please wait a moment.
One moment, please.
간결/자연
I will be late a little.
I’ll be a little late.
어순
I’m agree.
I agree.
불필요한 be
I can’t understand.
I’m not sure I understand.
부드럽게
Please tell me the detail.
Could you share more details?
복수형/톤
Let’s discuss about it.
Let’s discuss it.
about 불필요
I will inform to you.
I’ll let you know.
자연 표현
I’m sorry for inconvenience.
Sorry for the inconvenience.
관사/구
Please kindly…
Could you please…
과한 “kindly” 주의
We need to check it carefully.
We’ll review it carefully.
업무 표현
I will share you soon.
I’ll share it with you soon.
목적어/전치사
If you have time, please…
When you have a moment, could you…
자연/정중

팁: 위 “After” 문장들을 그대로 템플릿으로 외워두면, AI 없이도 기본 톤이 확 좋아져요.
